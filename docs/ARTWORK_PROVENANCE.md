# Grounded artwork provenance

On 2026-09-19, the Grounded project owner supplied four illustrations and
stated that they generated the images with ChatGPT for this project. The owner
authorized their use in Grounded.

| Asset | Intended context |
|---|---|
| `assets/grounded-field-home.png` | Rural resilience and the living-twin workspace |
| `assets/grounded-monsoon.png` | Monsoon and climate-risk workspaces |
| `assets/grounded-clover-sky.png` | Strategy and counterfactual-proof workspaces |
| `assets/grounded-clover-studio.png` | Evidence and methodology workspace |

These images are illustrative artwork. They do not depict the named Jaipur
facility, its equipment, measured telemetry, or a partner organization. The
application displays an "ILLUSTRATIVE ARTWORK" disclosure wherever the images
appear. Grounded's factual claims remain tied to the evidence ledger and cited
sources, not to these images.

## Expanded live-system city

The city retains the code-native isometric rendering pipeline supplied in the
owner's `claude-clan-main.zip`: the 96 × 48 grid, roads, airport, harbour,
courthouse, building primitives, construction crews and crane/scaffold sequence.
The detailed hospital, school, temple, church, Eiffel-inspired observation
tower, wind turbines, electricity pylons, bus/car textures and offshore forest
are Grounded-authored extensions to that pipeline. They are not assets claimed
to have existed in the supplied archive.

These are illustrative structures, not surveyed Jaipur infrastructure. The
wind rotors and traffic are ambient animations, not live operational telemetry.
Decorative forest trees are separate from inspectable evidence trees, which
link to completed simulation runs. The bridge adds a connection across the
existing water channel without cutting away the mainland terrain.
