## Project title

Grounded — Test Tomorrow Before It Arrives

## One-line description

A climate-resilience digital twin that explains how critical infrastructure
fails and proves low-disruption interventions on unseen futures before money is
spent.

## Short description

Grounded helps communities test a solar-battery microgrid against compound
heat, cloud, demand, EV and outage conditions. It simulates 72 hours at
15-minute resolution, identifies the exact chain that interrupts hospital
power, searches 245 operating policies and replays the same failed future to
prove whether the recommended intervention survives. Every run is seeded,
auditable and reproducible in the browser.

## Full description

Climate-resilience plans are often judged by averages and tested only after an
emergency. Grounded turns that process into a reproducible experiment.

The public application combines Jaipur climate calibration from NASA POWER
with a clearly labelled measured operational reference containing 34,944
quarter-hour demand readings, more than 5.25 million PV intervals and 9,210
historical outage/restoration events. Each simulated future carries solar,
grid, battery, hospital, household and EV state through 288 dispatch intervals.
Grounded exposes critical-risk uncertainty, LOLP, LOLE, EENS, tail severity,
renewable provenance, operational carbon and a timestamped causal failure chain.

Its optimizer exhaustively evaluates 245 reserve, EV-delay and pre-cooling
strategies across five hazards. The winner is tested on separate seeds, three
disjoint holdouts, exact paired inference, four individual assumption shocks,
an 81-cell compound stress envelope and historical climate periods. Every
Pareto finalist is independently reranked on the holdouts so judges can see the
selected policy's rank, competing winner and maximum regret rather than accept
an unexplained recommendation.

Grounded is decision support, not certified engineering software. The default
operational data is not presented as Jaipur facility telemetry. A documented
SMS Hospital solar installation provides the real local problem anchor, while
the built-in commissioning workflow quality-gates and fingerprints authorized
local demand, PV and outage exports when they become available.

## Earth Forward connection

Grounded advances climate adaptation and clean-energy resilience by helping
communities use solar and storage more intelligently during compound extreme
events. It connects environmental performance with a human outcome: protecting
critical hospital electricity while quantifying curtailment, renewable energy
served, grid carbon, cost and service risk.

## What makes it original

Most dashboards predict weather or display energy totals. Grounded links
climate calibration, measured operational profiles, interval dispatch physics,
causal failure explanation, multi-objective optimization and same-seed
counterfactual proof in one auditable workflow. It also tests whether the
recommendation survives compound assumption changes and remains competitive
against its Pareto alternatives on unseen cohorts.

## Technology

TypeScript, React, Vite, Web Workers, deterministic Monte Carlo simulation,
15-minute microgrid dispatch, NASA POWER, measured demand/PV/outage reference
data, Pareto optimization, exact McNemar paired inference, Wilson intervals,
interpretable logistic-surrogate diagnostics, SQLite server persistence,
Playwright, Vitest, GitHub Actions and Vercel.

## Evidence boundary
