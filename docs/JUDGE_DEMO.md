# Grounded — three-minute judge demonstration

## One-sentence thesis

Grounded lets a community replay thousands of climate-stressed operating
futures, diagnose the precise chain that interrupts critical infrastructure,
and prove a low-disruption intervention on unseen futures before money is
spent.

## 0:00–0:25 — establish the problem and the evidence boundary

Open **Method** briefly. Point to the climate fingerprint, operational-data
fingerprint, measured 15-minute demand/PV profiles, 9,210 outage records and
blocked holdout results.

Say: “This demo combines Jaipur climate observations with a disclosed measured
Australian operational reference. It is not falsely presented as Jaipur meter
data. A real site can be commissioned below from three CSV exports.”

## 0:25–0:55 — create a failure worth solving

Return to **Living twin**, choose **Extreme Combined Event**, select **500
futures**, and run.

Say: “Every future is a deterministic 72-hour dispatch at 15-minute resolution.
The seeds are fixed, so intervention comparisons cannot win through sample
luck.”

## 0:55–1:35 — explain, do not merely score

In **Risk evidence**, show:

1. critical probability and 95% Wilson interval;
2. 7/7 machine audit and run fingerprint;
3. LOLP, LOLE, EENS, P99 severity, CVaR95 and CVaR99;
4. the exact timestamped failure chain and binding constraint;
5. dominant-driver interaction, conservative renewable provenance and the
   operational carbon boundary;
6. the physics-informed ML audit: untouched-holdout AUROC, Brier score,
   calibration error and transparent driver weights. State clearly that the
   exact dispatch—not the surrogate—remains the authority.

## 1:35–2:20 — optimize without overfitting

Open **Strategy**, run the optimizer, and point out:

- 245 disclosed strategies across five hazards;
- independent 300-future discovery cohort;
- three disjoint holdouts and four assumption shocks;
- the Pareto frontier across risk, energy, disruption, cost and carbon;
- dated NASA climate replay.

## 2:20–2:45 — counterfactual proof

Open **Proof** and select the failed seed. Show the identical future before and
after. Only the intervention may change.

Say: “This is not a different lucky scenario. It is the same failure seed,
replayed with the recommended operating policy.”

## 2:45–3:00 — prove transferability

Open **Method → Local commissioning**.

Say: “Demand, PV and outage exports become a quality-gated site twin. Grounded
withholds 20%, fingerprints every normalized input, and marks weak data for
review rather than manufacturing certainty.”

End with: “Grounded turns resilience from a promise into a reproducible test.”

## 45-second fallback

1. Show Method fingerprints and holdout PASS.
2. Show a prepared Extreme run with 7/7 checks and the ML holdout audit.
3. Show the failure chain.
4. Show same-seed before/after proof.
5. End on the commissioning panel.
