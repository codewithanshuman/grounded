# Grounded — 4:25 judge pitch

This script is intentionally under five minutes at a calm speaking pace. The
deck carries the same facts in its speaker notes. Do not improvise a formal
partnership claim: the current collaboration stage is **public evidence / outreach
ready**, not acknowledged or commissioned.

## 0:00–0:30 — Slide 1: the outcome

“Climate resilience is usually tested after the emergency. Grounded lets a
hospital test tomorrow before it arrives. It runs the energy system through
hundreds of reproducible heat, cloud, demand and outage futures, explains the
exact chain behind every critical failure, and proves the smallest operating
change on futures the optimizer never saw.”

## 0:30–1:10 — Slide 2: a real Jaipur anchor

“This is not a fictional Jaipur problem. A published 2025–26 impact assessment
documents a 70-kWp solar plant at Rajkiya Dharamshala, SMS Hospital. A hospital
representative reported roughly 80 percent demand coverage and a monthly bill
reduction from about ninety thousand to forty thousand rupees. That establishes
a real facility and intervention. It does not provide 15-minute meters or prove
islanded outage performance—and Grounded says that openly.”

## 1:10–1:50 — Slide 3: what the engine actually does

“Grounded combines Jaipur NASA POWER climate observations with a disclosed,
measured Ausgrid operational reference: quarter-hour demand, measured PV and
9,210 outage/restoration records. It dispatches solar, grid and battery through
288 15-minute intervals for each future. Every run is deterministic, every
source is fingerprinted, and the public site executes the engine in the browser,
so judging does not depend on a fragile backend.”

## 1:50–2:40 — Slide 4: explanation, not a score

“In the extreme run, 15 of 500 futures lose hospital power: 3.0 percent, with a
95 percent interval of 1.8 to 4.9. Grounded does not stop at that percentage. It
shows the first loss timestamp, energy balance, LOLP, LOLE, EENS, CVaR tail risk
and the full causal chain. A compact held-out ML surrogate reaches 1.000 AUROC,
but it is diagnostic only. Exact physics remains the authority, and seven of
seven machine invariants pass.”

## 2:40–3:30 — Slide 5: proof against overfitting

“Grounded exhaustively searches 245 reserve, EV-delay and pre-cooling policies
across five hazards on an independent 300-future cohort. The selected policy—20
percent reserve, 180-minute EV delay and 11 a.m. pre-cooling—takes the original
500-future baseline from 15 critical failures to zero. It then passes three
disjoint holdouts, four assumption shocks, and prevents seven holdout failures
without introducing one. Same seeds before and after mean the policy cannot win
by getting an easier scenario.”

## 3:30–4:05 — Slide 6: the collaboration truth

“We are at the public-evidence and outreach-ready stage. We have a named Jaipur
facility, a source-checked field assessment, a minimum data contract and a
review packet for the hospital operations team and Aavas Foundation. We do not
claim a partnership before someone replies. The next evidence step is a named
reviewer and permission for 30 days of 15-minute demand, PV and outage data;
then Grounded quality-gates and fingerprints a commissioned site twin.”

## 4:05–4:25 — Slide 7: the ask

“The public demo is live and reproducible. Our ask is small: a 20-minute
technical review and three non-sensitive exports covering 30 days. Give Grounded
one real boundary and one real outage history, and we will turn resilience from
a promise into a test. Make Jaipur’s next outage boring.”
