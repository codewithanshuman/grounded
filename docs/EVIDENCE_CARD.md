# Grounded evidence card

## Claims Grounded can support

- A disclosed microgrid configuration was evaluated across a reproducible set
  of correlated 72-hour operating futures.
- The reported critical-risk estimate, confidence interval, reliability
  metrics and causal traces were generated by executable dispatch physics.
- A recommended intervention was searched on independent seeds and evaluated
  on the baseline population, three holdouts, assumption shocks and dated
  climate replay.
- Holdout and shock comparisons are paired by seed, explicitly counting both
  prevented failures and any failures introduced by the policy.
- Statistical depth includes Wilson uncertainty, probability of any unserved
  load, P99 severity, CVaR99 and a two-driver interaction replay.
- An interpretable diagnostic surrogate is trained on 80% of completed
  simulations and scored on an untouched 20% holdout using AUROC, Brier score,
  calibration error and balanced accuracy. Its standardized coefficients
  summarize simulator behavior; they are not causal claims.
- Selected operational data passed the displayed completeness and holdout
  gates, and its fingerprint is bound to the run.

## Claims Grounded does not make

- The Ausgrid public reference is not Jaipur commissioning data.
- A successful simulation is not a protection study, load-flow study, safety
  certification, procurement approval or guarantee of uninterrupted service.
- Avoided grid emissions are operational estimates, not lifecycle accounting
  or carbon offsets.
- The model does not currently represent feeder topology, voltage/frequency,
  harmonics, relay coordination or component-level network failures.
- Surrogate holdout performance is not evidence of real-world predictive
  accuracy, and the ML layer does not replace the physics engine.

## Current measured reference

- Demand: 34,944 15-minute readings, 99.73% complete.
- PV: 5,252,112 half-hour gross-generation intervals from 300 systems, 99.93%
  complete and normalized to 15 minutes.
- Reliability: 9,210 outage/restoration-duration events plus audited annual
  SAIFI/SAIDI.
- Blocked holdout: 72 demand days, 73 PV days and 1,842 outage events withheld.
- Current source fingerprint: `SITE-68A008486D22`.
